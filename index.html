About

I'm Walker, a finance analyst based in Noblesville, Indiana. Most days I'm working with sales and distribution data: building the reporting, forecasts and incentive compensation tools that leadership uses to make decisions.

However, my path here wasn't a straight line. I served six years in the Army National Guard (2 reserver) as a meteorological surveyor and a telecommunications and networking specialist. I've sold and supported tech at Microsoft and Apple, taught Microsoft Office classes to kids and adults, ran a STEM program for kids, and spent four years as an IT and labor-management coordinator at a logistics company, where I found out how much I like making data useful.

Outside of work I still write code. I started with JavaScript back in 2017 and these days I build small apps and websites for myself, my family and friends. A few of them are in my Fun Projects section.
